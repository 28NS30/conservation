# W10 — Supporting and system pages, loading and error states, share images

Paths relative to `apps/web`; code read at `c73bb8d`. Read `AGENTS.md`, `../direction.md` §2.5–2.6, `../inventory/supporting.md`.

**Goal**
A stranger opening /about learns in the first screen what this is, who is responsible, and that today's 46,334 records were collected by 路殺社 (TaiRON) volunteers — before any talk of blurring; /privacy and /attribution become scannable documents set at 16px instead of 14px walls. A 404 or a thrown error keeps the header, footer, mark and language switch, says the same thing in both 404 files, and gives an English visitor an English way out. Skeletons match the redesigned pages and speak Chinese; a shared link renders a card in the new skin.

**Why / evidence**
- `error-links` — `[locale]/not-found.tsx:12-14` and `[locale]/error.tsx:34-39` link `/` under a "back to map" label (W0c fixes the string) and offer no `/map`. - The two 404s diverge for no reason — 30px vs 36px numeral, different body, `px-4 py-1.5` vs `px-5 py-2`, different action pairs (`not-found.tsx:8-17` vs `global-not-found.tsx:34-54`) — and `global-not-found.tsx:10,43-54` is Chinese-only in title and actions, so `/en/typo` drops an English reader into zh.

- /about names no organisation and no source: the lede reuses `site("description")` (`about/page.tsx:59`), four blocks carry identical weight, and the only identity is a 112px badge lettered PROJECT ECOWATCH plus a personal gmail (`:42`), in a 672px column inside 1440px of beige (`shots/about-fold.png`).

- Share and install identity is off-palette: `#fb7185` at `species/[id]/opengraph-image.tsx:45,179`, tracked Hanzi at `opengraph-image.tsx:116`, `#0b1410` on a light site at `manifest.ts:20-21`, and `sitemap.ts:41-48` omits /about, /attribution, /privacy, /stats.

**Design spec**

*Shared prose shape.* Every supporting page is `PageTitle` + `Prose` at `--container-measure` (680px): `text-body` prose, `text-head` sub-headings, sections opened by a 2px `--rule`. Roundel adds one plate `Section` per page; Field journal uses the rule alone — the only structural difference between the directions here.

*/about, desktop.* A plate `Section` hero of about 520px: emblem 300px left; right column h1 at `text-title` and one `text-lead` sentence answering "what is this" instead of the SEO description. Then on paper at 680px: **誰在做這件事 / Who runs this** (new), then 這是什麼 (W0c's rewritten `about.whatBody`), 為什麼有些位置是模糊的, 還沒辨識出來的通報, 怎麼參與, 聯絡. Close with `LinkAction`s to /attribution and /privacy and one `Button` 通報一筆; the three foot pills (`:82-101`) go. *Phone:* same order, emblem 200px centred.

*/privacy.* `PageTitle`, a `text-note` 最後更新 line, a jump list to eight `id`-bearing `<h2>`s, the existing copy unchanged at `text-body`, contact as a `Notice` naming the responsible person and the mailto — 個資法 needs both reachable.

*/attribution.* Same shell, live query untouched. Heads `text-note` 700 over a 2px rule, cells `text-body`, counts `tabular-nums`, rows stacked two-line below 640px. Render the total row only when `licenses.length > 1` (today it duplicates the single row).

*/team.* Composition ported to the new spacing and `text-lead` names. `TEAM` stays `[]`; the page keeps 404ing.

*Status pages.* One `components/site/StatusPage.tsx` — no `"use client"`, not async, no cookies, so the client `error.tsx` can import it: mark or emblem, code at `text-title` `tabular-nums`, title at `text-title`, one `text-body` sentence, optional digest at `text-note`, and two 48px actions passed in as a prop so `global-not-found` can hand it plain `<a>`s. Put the boundaries inside the chrome: add `(site)/not-found.tsx` and `(site)/error.tsx` so `(site)/layout.tsx` stays mounted (every `notFound()` caller — `species/[id]`, `reports/[id]`, `team` — is in that group), keeping the `[locale]` files as bare fallbacks. `[locale]/not-found.tsx` gains 查看地圖 → `/map`, mirroring `global-not-found.tsx:49-54`.

**Risks and traps**
- **The loading boundary is load-bearing.** `Skeleton.tsx:13-23`: "Any route beneath it that calls redirect() or notFound() then answers 200 instead". Add no `loading.tsx` to `/species/[id]`, `/reports/[id]`, `/team` or a parent, and re-check status codes after step 5.
- **Privacy.** /about's count goes through `asPublic` over `reports_public`, like `attribution/page.tsx:37-46`. Keep the promise, not the mechanism (`about/page.tsx:24-29`): no roles, no offset internals.
- **/team.** `lib/team.ts:8-12`: "The roster is EMPTY and must stay that way until the team sends real content." No placeholder person, no stock face; do not add `/team` to the sitemap.
- **Deliberate code that looks wrong.** `global-not-found.tsx:1-5`: "next/link would ship one to a page whose whole point is being served without it" — keep plain `<a>` and its own `globals.css` import. `global-error.tsx:3-7`: "Deliberately dependency-free". `Badge.tsx:12-15`: `rounded-full` is real work, "without clipping it shows four white corners".
- **Shared files.** `messages/*.json` (append keys, rebase, never reformat — CI enforces parity); `e2e/pages.spec.mjs` `PAGES`/`NAMESPACES` (one-line appends; several workstreams touch them); `globals.css` (W2 only); `SiteHeader`/`SiteFooter` (W3). `PageHeader.tsx` dies in W2's closing PR — port to `PageTitle`, do not extend it.
- Enlarging the emblem enlarges the stale ECOWATCH lettering (decision 5); the redraw is on the team's critical path.
