# W7 — Species directory and species detail

Paths relative to `apps/web` (base c73bb8d). Read `AGENTS.md` first. Companions: `../direction.md` §2.5 and §4, `../inventory/species.md`, briefs `W2.md` (primitives) and `W0d.md` (behaviour fixes landing first).

**Goal**
`/species` becomes a browsable index — search, status and group filters, paging, real hierarchy — where all 354 recorded species are reachable without searching. A species page opens with the animal's name at hero scale, says in one sentence what we know (how many records, which years, which month peaks), then a portrait map and a seasonality chart, and ends with one action that reports *that* species. Pages with no records, or with coordinates withheld, look deliberate and never say nobody has reported anything.

**Why / evidence**
- `directory-cap` (`(directory)/page.tsx:51-55`): W0d adds paging; the wall of 80 identical beige boxes (`:97-127`; `shots/species-dir-full.png`, 3,909px desktop, ~7,060px phone) is ours. Name, count and status all whisper at 14/12/10px.
- `tiny-text`: chips 10px (`StatusBadges.tsx:141`), lineage 11px (`species/[id]/page.tsx:176`), chart labels 9px (`MonthlyChart.tsx:30`), notices 11-12px (`:186,220`) — the legally meaningful facts are the least legible things on the page.
- Pills everywhere: filters (`(directory)/page.tsx:76`), habitat (`HabitatChips.tsx:49`), status, map toggle, both CTAs (`species/[id]/page.tsx:194,263`) — actions, filters, facts and status look alike, the language the owner rejected.
- Map: `SpeciesMap.tsx:106-116` pads 52% right, so desktop is an island in the left third of a 720×320 black slab and the phone crops the west coast and the south (`shots/species-detail-phone-full.png`). No legend; wheel and thumb captured (`lib/map.ts`, `navigation:false`); dots `#34d399` (`:244`) off-palette; `dark-tokens-on-light`.
- Banned sentence `species.noRecords`/`beFirst` (`species/[id]/page.tsx:189-198`) on ~65,850 on-demand pages; W0d changed only its href.
- Dead ends: `/map?taxonId=` and `/reports?taxonId=` exist (`map/page.tsx:97`, `reports/(list)/page.tsx:101`) and nothing links to them; lineage is plain text.
- `/en` is second-class: binomial h1 upright, Hanzi secondary italicised (`species/[id]/page.tsx:151`, `(directory)/page.tsx:114`) — `opengraph-image.tsx:138` already comments that italic Hanzi is wrong. `species.records` reads 通報紀錄 / "Records" for 100% imported TaiRON roadkill, with no source line on the page.

**Design spec**
Skin through W2 roles and primitives, never literal colours. The directions differ structurally in one place: Roundel's hero is a `Section surface="plate"` band, Field journal's is a 2px rule with a margin heading on the page ground.

*Directory, 1440.* `Container`, `PageTitle` 物種名錄 at `text-title`, lede at `text-body`. Controls: a 56px `Field type="search"` inside `<form method="get">` (works without JS) carrying hidden `filter`/`group`; two `Filter` rows of underlined words, not pills — status (有紀錄 · 全部 · 保育類 · 入侵種 · 特有種) and group — `aria-current` on the active word; W0d's range line at `text-note`. One-column `List` of `DataRow`s, 64px min, whole row the link: name `text-lead` 700 with **no `truncate`** (wrap — this is the field people use to tell species apart), `Binomial` at `text-body`, `StatusTag` glyph-plus-words instead of chips, count right-aligned at `text-lead` `tabular-nums` with `recordsShort` on the same line. With `?group=`, `text-head` headings break the list. One column, not two: rows are tall enough now, and `h-full` (`:107`) goes with the grid. Empty: `EmptyState` naming the active filters, 清除篩選, and W0d's "search all species instead" path — never an absence sentence.
*Phone 390.* Same order, 16px gutters, controls ≤300px so four rows show at the fold; filter rows scroll sideways rather than wrapping into a pill cluster.

*Detail, desktop.* Hero: class linked at `text-note` to `/species?group=`, rest of the lineage plain; Chinese name at `text-hero` (six Hanzi or fewer) else `text-display`; `Binomial` at `text-lead`, authority upright; 也稱作 at `text-body`; `StatusTag`s for protection, endemism, invasiveness, sensitivity and habitat, one language, no pills. Under a 2px rule on paper, the **finding sentence** at `text-head`. Two columns: left a **portrait** map `Figure` 480×600 (Taiwan is tall; symmetric padding replaces the 52% east weighting), `Legend` beneath from W5's `legendFor()`, display `Filter` (圓點 · 方格 · 熱區, narrowed by `allowHeat`) above, `LinkAction`s to `/map?taxonId=` and `/reports?taxonId=`; right the monthly `Figure` and the lineage. Closing `Section surface="plate"`: 看到牠了？ at `text-title`, one `Button size="lg"` to `/report?taxonId=`, source line at `text-note`.
*Phone.* One column: name `text-display`/`text-title`, binomial, tags, finding sentence, map 358×440, chart 200px, full-width button.

*States.* Withheld (`座標不開放`): hero, a `Notice` with `coordinatesWithheld`, taxonomy, closing block; no map, count or chart. Zero records: hero, taxonomy, closing block. 1-5: dots map plus rows whose link is the date at `text-body`, coordinates at `text-note` with a `StatusTag` 位置已模糊化 replacing the bare `≈`. 6-39: chart plus map without heat. ≥40: full. Blurred-but-mapped: a `Notice` at `text-body`, not an 11px amber whisper.

*Map behaviour.* Cooperative gestures: `scrollZoom` and one-finger `dragPan` off until the canvas is clicked (overlay 點一下以操作地圖), released on blur — a wheel or thumb passing over must scroll the page. `jumpTo` under `prefers-reduced-motion`. Keep the shared mode preference and the `allowHeat` fallback exactly; colours from W5's `mapTheme`.

*Copy, all needing a native read.* `finding`: {from}–{to} 年間有 {count, number} 筆紀錄，{peak} 月最多。/ "{count, number} records between {from} and {to}, most in {peakMonth}." Plus `records` 紀錄 / Records; `source` 紀錄來源：路殺社（TaiRON），CC BY 4.0; `seenTitle` 看到牠了？/ "Seen one?"; `reportThis` 通報這個物種 / "Report this species"; `approxLocation` 位置已模糊化 / "Location blurred"; `onFullMap` 在完整地圖上看; `mapInteract` 點一下以操作地圖; `groupLabel` 依類群 and `group.*`. Delete `noRecords`, `beFirst`, `seenOne`; reuse `seeAllRecords`.

**Risks and traps**
- Privacy: every read stays on `asPublic` + `species_report_stats`/`reports_public` (`lib/species.ts:36-41`); never count from `reports`; the map keeps `/api/tiles?taxonId=` so blurring is inherited; sparse rows print `location_public` only.
- Deliberate, do not "fix": `SpeciesMap.tsx:87-92` "maxBounds fights the fitBounds below… the island came out cropped at the bottom" — keep `bounded: false`. `map-bins.test.mjs`: "What must not come back is heat being available — or worse, chosen — for a species with almost no records." `(directory)/page.tsx:120-121` count and 筆 on one line: "the lone 筆 read as a stray glyph". `HabitatChips` draws nothing when all four flags are NULL: "'we do not know' is not 'it lives nowhere'". `SpeciesCard.tsx:98-101` "records", never "rarity". Do not reorder the search ranking (`lib/species.ts:180-208`) while adding `group`.
- Scale: ~400 pages prerendered, ~65,850 on demand. The layout must look finished with zero badges, zero habitat, zero records and no Chinese name — screenshot that case.
- Shared files: `messages/*.json` (append inside `species` only), `e2e/pages.spec.mjs` (W4, W13), `lib/species.ts` (W0d, W6, W8), `HabitatChips.tsx` and `createMap` (W5), `MonthlyChart.tsx` (W9), `design-debt.json`. Warn W5 before changing the `tone` prop and W9 before touching the chart internals; never touch `stats/Columns.tsx`.
- A Tailwind class with no `--color-*` token emits nothing: use W2 roles and run the guard before assuming a colour applied. There is no imagery and none is licensed — design no slot that looks broken empty; the name is the picture.
